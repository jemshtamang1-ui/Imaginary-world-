Hey, have you ever looked at the night sky and wondered what would happen if every single star suddenly disappeared? I mean EVERY star! Gone! Just like that! At first, you might think the universe had gone completely dark. But wait, here's the crazy part. You wouldn't even notice immediately! You see, light takes time to travel through space. Some stars are so far away that their light takes hundreds or even thousands of years to reach us. So even if those stars disappeared, we could STILL see their light travelling towards Earth! But imagine the moment that final light disappears. No stars. No constellations. Just an endless, terrifying black sky. And here's something even crazier. Our SUN is a star too! If the Sun disappeared as well, Earth would lose its main source of light and heat. Temperatures would begin falling, and life as we know it would face a disaster. So let me ask you. Would you be able to sleep peacefully knowing that the entire universe above you had gone DARK?
